Methodology

The Rate Pressure Index is a sourced indicator for whether data-center-related grid buildout is likely adding pressure to electricity rates in a utility territory. It is not a determination that any individual customer's bill changed because of a data center.

Formula

Composite score = grid constraint x 25% + tariff protection x 25% + buildout scale x 20% + rate case x 20% + rate trend x 10%.

  • Grid constraint: market or system evidence that supply, capacity, or transmission is tightening.
  • Tariff protection: whether large-load customers have binding minimum bills, contract terms, collateral, or exit fees.
  • Buildout scale: the scale of data center or large-load demand in the service territory.
  • Rate case: recent or pending regulatory actions affecting customer rates.
  • Rate trend: whether customer-facing rate movement is above or below national/state baseline context.

Bands

0-30 Low, 31-55 Moderate, 56-75 Elevated, 76-100 High.

Limitations

The inputs are proxies, not ground truth. EIA Form 861 service territory data is county-based, so this v1 uses a conservative pilot ZIP lookup rather than claiming complete ZIP-level coverage. Correlation is not causation, and source quality varies by factor; where judgment is required, the score should be revisited as new filings appear.

Usage Estimates

The low, average, and high usage selector scales only a published average monthly bill impact or customer benefit. It is a rough illustration, not a precise bill forecast and not a claim that a data center caused that amount.
